Instruction to use

To achieve remarkable results with our Plastic Emulsion paint, begin by preparing your walls properly. Make sure they are clean, dry, and devoid of any peeling paint or dust. After that, give the paint a good stir to ensure it's well-mixed before you start applying it. Use a high-quality brush or roller to achieve a smooth finish, and don't forget to apply two coats for the best coverage. Allow the first coat to dry completely before moving on to the second one.For optimal results, it's best to paint in a well-ventilated area and steer clear of extreme temperatures during application. This will help the paint adhere better and dry evenly. Once you're done, step back and admire your beautifully transformed space, knowing that you've put in the effort to create a stunning environment with our high-quality paint!
